Our Story

22 years in the making

Bodytreat is the life's work of Rosie — a qualified aromatherapist who began her journey in England in 2002. After co-running a holistic health clinic in Hereford for a decade, she returned to New Zealand and settled in the beautiful Tasman region near Nelson.

Every product is blended by hand in small batches, using locally sourced New Zealand botanicals and pure essential oils. No shortcuts, no compromise — just genuine therapeutic quality in every bottle.
