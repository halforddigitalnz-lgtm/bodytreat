About this blend

A supportive and nurturing blend crafted with hormonal balance in mind. Each essential oil in this formula has been chosen by Rosie for its specific affinity with the female body — Clary Sage and Sweet Fennel for hormonal support, Bergamot to lift the spirit, and the florals of Jasmine and Rose Geranium to soften and calm. A deeply personal blend from over two decades of aromatherapy practice.

How to use

Massage into the lower abdomen, back, or any area of discomfort to help ease cramps and tension. Alternatively, add 20ml to a warm bath for a full aromatic experience. Use throughout the month as a nourishing skin treatment. Store in a cool place out of direct sunlight. External use only.

Full ingredients

Sweet Almond Oil, Grapeseed Oil, Macadamia Oil, essential oils of Jasmine (Jasmine officinale), Rose Geranium (Pelargonium graveolens), Clary Sage (Salvia sclarea), Bergamot (Citrus aurantium spp bergamia), Sweet Fennel (Foeniculum vulgare), Vitamin E (natural mixed tocopherols)
